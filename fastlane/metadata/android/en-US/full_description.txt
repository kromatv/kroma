Your library deserves a beautiful home.

KROMA brings your own movies and series together in a free, open-source app for Android phones, tablets and Android TV. Connect to your KROMA server and make your next movie night feel effortless.

EXPLORE YOUR COLLECTION
Browse your movies and series with rich artwork, descriptions and cast information. Find a favorite with search, discover what is in your library and keep a personal watchlist for later.

AT HOME ON EVERY SCREEN
Enjoy an interface designed for the screen in front of you: touch navigation on your phone or tablet, and remote-control navigation on Android TV and Google TV.

KEEP YOUR PLACE
Continue watching with playback progress saved to your KROMA account. Move between supported devices connected to the same server and account.

TAKE YOUR LIBRARY WITH YOU
On phones and tablets, download supported media for offline viewing. Available playback options depend on your server, your media and your device.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
KROMA is built in the open. Explore the project, share feedback or contribute at https://github.com/kromatv/kroma.

BRING YOUR OWN SERVER AND MEDIA
This app is a client for a KROMA media server. You need access to a compatible KROMA server and your own media library. KROMA does not include movies, series, TV channels or streaming subscriptions. A network connection to your server is required for streaming and library access; remote access depends on your server setup.
