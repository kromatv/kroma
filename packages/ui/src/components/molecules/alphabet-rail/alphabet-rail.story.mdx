The NavPill turned vertical: a fast-scroll rail for a long alphabetical list. The **amber lens** covers every letter whose section is on screen (a tall viewport shows several at once) and travels with the scroll; scrubbing along the rail shows a bubble naming the letter under the finger and jumps as it crosses rows. A letter the list does not carry is `disabled`: it renders dimmed, takes no focus stop, and a scrub or tap near it snaps to its nearest present neighbour. The host owns the list and the scroll: the rail only reports jumps.

One `<AlphabetRail.Item>` per bucket, in rail order, is the whole API: the item says which letter it stands for and whether the list carries it, and the Root reads their positions to place the lens.

```tsx
<AlphabetRail.Root
  label={t('browse.letterNav')}
  range={visible}            // {first, last}: the sections on screen
  onJump={scrollToLetter}
>
  {TITLE_LETTERS.map((letter) => (
    <AlphabetRail.Item
      key={letter}
      value={letter}
      disabled={!lettersInView.has(letter)}
      label={t('browse.jumpToLetter', { letter })}
    />
  ))}
</AlphabetRail.Root>
```
