The multi-line entry, under `<Field.Textarea>`, and the same two spellings as `<TextField>`: a live `TextInput` where a keyboard exists, a display plus a caret on a television, so nothing there can be tapped into and summon the platform IME. It opens `rows` tall and **grows with what is typed**, up to `maxRows`, past which it scrolls instead of pushing the rest of the form off the screen. The growth is the browser's own `field-sizing: content` on the web, where a measured callback would cost a state write per keystroke for the same layout, and a measured content height on native. The box aligns its content to the TOP rather than the centre, because a field that grows downwards has to keep its first line where the reader left it, and the whole box, padding included, is the caret's landing zone.

```tsx
<Field.Root label="Notes">
  <Field.Textarea rows={4} value={notes} onValueChange={setNotes} />
  <Field.Hint>Grows as you type, up to ten lines.</Field.Hint>
</Field.Root>
```

<Scene name="One line, beside a single-line field">
  A row is the **md** shell's content line, whatever `size` says, and that is what makes a one-line textarea exactly a `md` `<Field.Input>` tall: the two below share an edge. Take both to `sm` and they part company, because the field shrinks its line to 20 and the textarea keeps counting 24s.

<Scene name="Past the cap">
  More text than `maxRows` allows: the box stops growing and the content scrolls inside it. That cap is the whole reason the prop exists. Without it a pasted paragraph walks the submit button off the bottom of the screen.

  <Box gap={10}>

<Do>
- Reach it through `<Field.Textarea>`: the label row, the hint and the error come with it.
- Set `rows` to the length the answer usually is, and `maxRows` to the point past which the form would be pushed off the screen.
- Set a `lineHeight` alongside any bigger font in `textStyle`. Lines are what `rows` counts, so a font size on its own makes the opening height a lie.
</Do>

<Dont>
- Use it for a single line, or for a value with a shape (a URL, an amount): that is `<Field.Input>`.
- Switch `autoSize` off to keep a form still. `maxRows` already bounds the growth, and a fixed box starts scrolling under a reader who is mid-sentence.
- Assume `rows` follows `size`: a row is the md shell's line at every size, so a `sm` textarea stands taller than the `sm` field beside it.
</Dont>
