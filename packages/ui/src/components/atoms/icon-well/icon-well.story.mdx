The leading mark of a row: a glyph sunk into a rounded, tinted square. A well rather than a bare glyph because it gives a column of icons ONE optical weight: a hollow gear and a solid television differ by half their ink, and the wells line them up anyway. `<ListRow>` draws its own; reach for this one when you are building a row the kit does not have.

```tsx
<IconWell name="server-2" />
<IconWell name="plus" tone="accent" size="sm" />
```
