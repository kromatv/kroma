The darkening laid over full-screen art (a backdrop, a hero) so the title and the rows standing on it read. It takes the same `linear-gradient(...)` list `background-image` does, the first on top, and fades to the page ground rather than to the art.

On the browser targets the ramp is drawn once into a small bitmap and stretched over the box: a television rasterizes in software, and a full-screen gradient there is two million pixels to paint again whenever anything near it changes, while a stretched image is one upload the GPU scales. React Native draws the gradient itself.

```tsx
<Box fill>
  <Img src={backdrop} fill layered />
  <Veil ramp={`${VERTICAL}, ${HORIZONTAL}`} />
</Box>
```
