The fade every tile lays over its artwork so the caption on it survives bright key art. Two ramps: `soft` leaves the picture alone until the bottom third, `deep` is already dark under a title that sits ON the art. The pale amber standing in for artwork here is the worst case a scrim has to work against.

```tsx
<Box aspect={16 / 9} radius="xl" overflow="hidden">
  <Img src={art} background={tintGradient(tint)} radius={16} fill />
  <ArtScrim variant="soft" radius="xl" />
  <Box absolute left={18} right={18} bottom={16}>
    <Text>{title}</Text>
  </Box>
</Box>
```
