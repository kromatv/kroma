The screen a film ends on. It replaces the chrome rather than covering it: there is no playhead left to scrub and no transport worth showing, so what is left is the film that just finished, the next one to watch, and the way out.

It arrives rather than cutting in: the art fades up and the copy settles into it over 420ms, which is the same easing every other travel in the player uses.

`focus` is the player's, not the screen's. Both buttons are controlled, because the chrome runs its own virtual focus and a second, real one would let a television's focus engine light a button this machine is not on.

`stageWidth` picks the type tier rather than scaling one: a stage 1600 wide or more is a television and takes the kit's 10-foot ladder, anything narrower is a desk and takes the desktop one. The layout does not otherwise change.
