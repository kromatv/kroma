What plays after this one. It has three states rather than two: parked (a **peek** of the sheet above the bottom edge, shown only when the chrome is revealed and there is actually something next), open (it rises and captures the D-pad), and gone. The peek is what makes it discoverable on a television, where there is no hover and no scrollbar to hint that something is below.
