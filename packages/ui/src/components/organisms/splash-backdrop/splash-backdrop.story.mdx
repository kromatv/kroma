The sign-in screens' ambient artwork, one implementation for web, phone and TV: covers dissolve on a slow drift under a grade dark enough to hold a form, with the **KROMA wheel as a 3px rule** along the bottom edge. Hosts fetch the public `/api/splash` sample, map it to covers, and drop this behind their gate. Decorative by contract: it swallows no pointer events and hides from accessibility.

```tsx
const covers = splash.map((e) => ({
  url: e.backdropUrl,
  caption: [e.title, e.year].filter(Boolean).join(' · '),
  eyebrow: t(e.kind === 'show' ? 'content.series' : 'content.film'),
}));

<SplashBackdrop covers={covers} />
```

- Give the host screen its own readable ground for text the grade alone cannot carry (fields and buttons already bring their fills).
- Centre the host content: the radial half of the grade is deepest where a form sits.
</Do>

<Dont>
- Feed it unsized artwork URLs; resolve them like any poster first.

- Draw over the bottom 3px; the wheel rule is the brand signature of these screens.
</Dont>
