The brand film every client opens with: a chromatic light tunnel landing on the KR-wheel-MA lockup, full-screen with sound. It ships as **4K60 HEVC only** - fitting for an HEVC-first product - and anything without a hardware decoder falls back to a pure-CSS scene that needs no codec at all.

It is a DOM component, deliberately: a real `<video>`, `document` visibility handling, and inline styles rather than a framework so it renders identically on the SSR shell and on a 2016 TV webview. **On native it renders `null`** - the Apple TV and phone clients play the same film through `expo-video` in their own shell, which is why this story is a note rather than a film in the native workbench.
