# Install KROMA on an Apple TV

KROMA for Apple TV is a beta, distributed through TestFlight, Apple's app for
testing apps before they reach the App Store. An Apple TV cannot open a link,
so you join the beta on an iPhone or iPad, then install from the Apple TV.

## Before you begin

- An Apple TV with tvOS 18 or later.
- An iPhone or iPad, signed in to the same Apple Account as the Apple TV.

## Join the beta on your iPhone or iPad

1. Install [TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) from
   the App Store.
2. Open this link on the iPhone or iPad:
   [testflight.apple.com/join/ZjgQdunW](https://testflight.apple.com/join/ZjgQdunW).
3. Select **Accept**.

The iPhone and the Apple TV share one beta: you can install KROMA on the phone
too, but you do not have to.

## Install on the Apple TV

1. On the Apple TV, open **TestFlight**. If it is not on the home screen,
   install it from the App Store.
2. Check that TestFlight uses the same Apple Account as your iPhone.
3. Select **KROMA**, then **Install**.

KROMA appears on the home screen. Open it, then follow
[Connect the TV to your server](tv.md#connect-the-tv-to-your-server).

## Updates

TestFlight installs each new version, or tells you when one is ready. A beta
version stops working 90 days after it was published, so keep KROMA updated.

## Troubleshooting

### The link says the beta is full or not available

The beta has a limited number of places, and each new version waits for Apple's
review. Try again later, or write to beta@kroma.tv.

### KROMA does not appear in TestFlight on the Apple TV

The Apple TV uses another Apple Account than the phone that joined. Sign in to
the same account in **Settings** > **Profiles and Accounts** on the Apple TV.
