Media libraries
===============

KROMA plays video through mpv and FFmpeg, which it ships beside the app:

  macOS    libmpv and every library it links (FFmpeg, libass, libplacebo,
           and their dependencies), copied from Homebrew into
           KROMA.app/Contents/Frameworks.
  Windows  libmpv-2.dll from shinchiro's mpv-winbuild-cmake, FFmpeg
           linked into it.
  Linux    the kroma-mpv program, pkgforge-dev's mpv AppImage.

mpv and FFmpeg are free software under the GNU General Public License,
version 2 or later. Some of the libraries they link are under the GNU Lesser
General Public License, and some FFmpeg components under version 3 of either.
Their source code:

  mpv          https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv
  FFmpeg       https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
  Homebrew     https://github.com/Homebrew/homebrew-core (the mpv and ffmpeg
               formulae name every library and the options they are built with)
  Windows      https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake
  Linux        https://github.com/pkgforge-dev/mpv-AppImage

The exact builds a release ships are pinned in
https://github.com/kromatv/kroma/tree/main/clients/desktop/scripts
(fetch-libmpv-windows.ps1, fetch-mpv.sh), and on macOS are the Homebrew
versions current when the release was built.
